About

Raised inside two filing systems

Being half Chinese and half American means I have been translating since before I could spell — not just words, but habits, tone and defaults. That is probably why I ended up in Information Science: file the same thing differently and suddenly people can find it.

My process is basically cataloguing: work out what exists, who is looking, and what words they would use — then design the interface. When the gap is big enough, I build it myself.
