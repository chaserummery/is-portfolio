Raised in Chongqing, studying in Champaign

Half Chinese, half American, raised in Chongqing, at UIUC since 2025. I have been translating between two languages and two sets of habits my whole life — probably why I ended up in Information Science: organize the same thing differently and suddenly people can find it.

My process: work out what exists, who is looking and what words they would use, then design the interface. When the gap is big enough I build it myself — that is how OrangeNest happened. Off-screen I manage and play for the Hotpot women's basketball team at UIUC, and make its posters.
